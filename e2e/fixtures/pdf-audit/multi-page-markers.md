## Writing Guidelines

Cut on sight: Em dashes used as a crutch, and stock phrasing such as "at the
end of the day," and equivalent stock phrasing. Summarizing closers — don't
recap what the paragraph just said, end on the last real point. Filler openers
— don't warm up into the topic, state it.
